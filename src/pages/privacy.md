We respect the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and this policy document explains how we collect and handle any information you provide to us.

## What background data is collected?

### Analytics (Plausible)
We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data for advertising. Plausible loads on every visit and is not gated by the cookie banner.

### Marketing (Google Tag Manager)
With your consent, we load Google Tag Manager (GTM). Tags configured in GTM (for example advertising or conversion pixels) may set cookies and process data for marketing measurement. Marketing cookies are opt-in worldwide: they load only after you accept in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time via the cookie preferences control in the footer.

### Newsletter (HubSpot)
See below.

## How do we use the data you provide?
Newsletter signup data is used only as described in the HubSpot section. Marketing tags (if you accept) are used for ads and conversion measurement as configured in GTM. Analytics via Plausible is used to understand aggregate site usage.

## Newsletter Subscription Data Processing: HubSpot
We use HubSpot to send our newsletter. The eMail addresses of our newsletter recipients as well as the other data processed when sending the newsletter are stored on HubSpot servers in the EU. Your data is used solely for sending newsletters and updates and will not be shared with third parties. You can unsubscribe at any time by following the link in our emails. You can find additional information about data protection at HubSpot at: https://legal.hubspot.com/privacy-policy.

## How secure is your data?
We minimise what we collect. Analytics via Plausible does not rely on advertising cookies. Marketing scripts load only with your consent.

## How secure is this website?
This website transmits itself using HTTPS, encrypting all data between the browser and the server. Your network provider can still see that you are downloading assets from this site, but not its content. This also protects you from third parties intercepting data, for example, while browsing on public WiFi, or from network providers injecting advertisements on this site. All relevant response HTTP headers are set for your protection and the Content Security Policy is adjusted to this websites requirements.
