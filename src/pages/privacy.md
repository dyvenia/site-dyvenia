## 1\. How and why we process your data

### 1.1 Website visitors

**Website operation and security.** When you visit our websites, technical information may be processed by our hosting and security infrastructure, such as your IP address, request time, requested page, browser and device information, and server logs. We process this information where necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the websites. The legal basis is our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

**Aggregate analytics – Plausible.** We use Plausible Analytics to understand aggregate website usage. Plausible does not set cookies or create persistent visitor identifiers. It processes limited technical data to generate aggregate statistics and does not store raw IP addresses. We use these statistics based on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving our websites (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Session analytics and user interaction – Microsoft Clarity.** We use Microsoft Clarity to analyze how users interact with our website through behavioral metrics, heatmaps, and session replay to improve and optimize our website design and user experience. Microsoft Clarity processes behavioral metrics, session details, and technical usage data using cookies and other tracking technologies. Data captured by Microsoft Clarity may be processed in accordance with Microsoft's privacy statement. The legal basis for using Microsoft Clarity is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, obtained via our consent banner.

**Optional technologies.** Technologies used for advertising measurement, remarketing, or optional embedded functionality are activated only where the required consent has been obtained. The legal basis for processing based on such consent is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can change your cookie/technology preferences at any time using the cookie preferences control available on our websites.

### 1.2 Contact, inquiries, and meeting bookings

If you contact us by email, through a website form, through social media, or book a meeting, we process the information you provide (for example, your name, business contact details, company, the content of your message, and meeting information) to respond, organize the meeting, and discuss potential cooperation. Depending on the context, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request before entering into a contract) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in handling business inquiries and correspondence).

### 1.3 Meta Lead Ads

We may use native lead forms provided by Meta on Facebook and Instagram. A form may ask for your name, surname, company, work email address, telephone number, and answers to business-related questions. Meta may verify a telephone number through its own SMS verification process. Dyvenia does not need the verification code itself.

If you submit a form to receive material such as an e-book or slide deck, we use the data to provide the requested material and handle the related inquiry. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to a request you initiated (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where your request directly concerns potential services, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR may also apply.

If you separately agree to direct marketing, we may also use your contact details to send marketing communications through the channel(s) covered by your consent. The legal basis for this processing is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Where Polish law applies, use of electronic communications or terminal equipment for direct marketing is also subject to Article 398 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law (Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej).

**Data flow.** Meta transfers submitted lead data to Dyvenia. Our HubSpot CRM and the separate lead-management panel at dyvenia.nexizen.pl import leads independently from Meta. These systems are not linked and do not exchange lead data.

### 1.4 Newsletter and direct marketing

If you opt in, we may send you information about Dyvenia's services, offers, events, webinars, publications, and other marketing content by the channel(s) you selected. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Consent is not a condition for receiving material that was offered without a marketing-consent requirement.

### 1.5 Business relationships and correspondence

If you are our client, supplier, contractor, or a representative or contact person of such an organization, we may process your business contact details and correspondence to manage the relationship, perform contracts, keep appropriate business records, comply with legal obligations, and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Depending on the context, the legal bases are Article 6(1)(b), (c), and/or (f) GDPR.

## 3\. Website technologies, cookies, and similar technologies

### 3.1 Necessary technologies

We may use technologies necessary to provide the websites, protect them against misuse, remember your privacy choices, and provide functionality you explicitly request. Where storage of or access to information on your device is strictly necessary to provide the service you requested, no optional-consent mechanism is required for that specific technology.

### 3.2 Plausible Analytics

Plausible is used for aggregate website statistics without cookies, local storage, or persistent visitor identifiers. Raw IP addresses are not stored by Plausible. Plausible visitor data is processed and stored in the European Union.

### 3.3 Microsoft Clarity

Microsoft Clarity provides behavioral analytics, session replays, and heatmaps to help us understand how users interact with our website. Microsoft Clarity uses cookies and tracking technologies to capture interaction data. Processing is conducted only after you consent via our banner (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

### 3.4 HubSpot

We use HubSpot for CRM, newsletters, and, on certain pages, meeting booking and related functions. If an embedded HubSpot function uses optional cookies or similar tracking technologies, it is loaded only after the relevant consent is given. Depending on the particular HubSpot feature and configuration, HubSpot may process technical information such as IP address, browser information, page interactions, and unique identifiers.

### 3.5 Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, and Meta Pixel

We use **Google Tag Manager** as a technical tag-management tool to organize and deploy various scripts and tags on our website (including Google Ads tags, Microsoft Clarity, and Meta Pixel).

**Google Ads.** We use Google Ads for conversion tracking, advertising measurement, and remarketing. Google Ads tags set cookies or read parameter identifiers (such as _gclid_) to track whether visits to our website lead to conversions (e.g., booked meetings or form submissions) and to display targeted advertising to users on Google Search and partner networks. Google Ads tags fire only after you provide marketing consent via our consent banner.

**Meta Pixel.** The Meta Pixel is configured as a marketing technology and is not allowed to fire before you consent to the marketing category. If you consent, the Meta Pixel may record: PageView (opening a page), Lead (the fact that a form was submitted), and Schedule (the fact that a call was booked). The form contents and the date, time, and details of the booked meeting are not sent to Meta through these events. Advanced Matching is disabled, so Dyvenia does not send your name, email address, or telephone number to Meta through the Pixel.

For these events, Meta and Google may receive the page URL and referrer, event time, IP address, browser and device information, and online identifiers. If you are logged in to Facebook, Instagram, or Google accounts in the same browser, the respective platform may be able to associate the event with your account through their own technologies.

## 7\. Your rights

We strive to make you happy by working with us. Remember, however, that you have (also if you come from the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland) many privileges that will allow you to influence the way we process your personal information and, in some cases, cause you to stop such processing. These rights are:

- **The right of access by the data subject (Article 15 GDPR):** You have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is being processed and obtain access to that data.
- **Right to rectification (Article 16 GDPR):** You have the right to obtain the rectification of inaccurate personal data concerning you without undue delay.
- **Right to erasure / 'right to be forgotten' (Article 17 GDPR):** You have the right to obtain the erasure of personal data concerning you under certain circumstances.
- **Right to restriction of processing (Article 18 GDPR):** You have the right to obtain restriction of processing where specific conditions apply.
- **Right to object to processing (Article 21 GDPR):** You have the right to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.
- **Right to data portability (Article 20 GDPR):** You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

To use any of the rights described, please contact us by email at <hello@dyvenia.com>.
